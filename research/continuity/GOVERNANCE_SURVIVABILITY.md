# Governance Survivability and Recovery Continuity

Governance durability depends not only on pre-deployment controls, but on the ability of governance systems to survive operational disruption, infrastructure failure, degraded communication environments, and continuity-preserving recovery scenarios without losing accountability visibility, intervention authority, or replay-capable oversight continuity.

Operational governance architectures should preserve replay-capable telemetry continuity, execution-state accountability records, and persistent human override availability during recovery operations, migration events, and post-incident reconstruction processes.

Recovery operations themselves constitute governance-relevant execution events. Continuity-preserving recovery actions generate replay-capable telemetry, restoration evidence, intervention traces, and accountability lineage records that become part of the operational governance evidence surface.

Policies describe intent. Runtime telemetry proves operation.
