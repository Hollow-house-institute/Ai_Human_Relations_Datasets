# Operational Governance Scenario

## Scenario
A coalition-operated autonomous intelligence analysis platform is deployed in a degraded operational environment during a regional communications disruption.

The system coordinates:
- retrieval systems
- multi-agent analysis pipelines
- prioritization models
- human review checkpoints
- escalation routing infrastructure

During execution, infrastructure instability causes:
- temporary telemetry interruption
- partial runtime migration
- delayed escalation delivery
- session recovery operations

## Governance Risk

Without execution-time governance infrastructure:
- escalation state may be lost
- replay continuity may break
- human override authority may disappear
- accountability attribution may become unverifiable
- post-incident audit reconstruction may fail

## Governance Continuity Requirements

The governance runtime preserves:
- replay-capable governance evidence
- telemetry continuity
- escalation persistence
- Stop Authority availability
- Responsibility Binding continuity
- intervention traceability

## Recovery Validation Sequence

Recovery actions generate:
- timestamped governance telemetry
- recovery-state transition events
- replay continuity validation records
- accountability restoration evidence
- governance continuity attestations

The recovery sequence itself becomes governance telemetry.

## Governance Outcome

The system resumes operation only after:
- escalation state reconciliation
- replay validation
- Stop Authority verification
- accountability rehydration
- governance continuity scoring

This transforms operational recovery from an infrastructure event into replay-capable governance evidence.
