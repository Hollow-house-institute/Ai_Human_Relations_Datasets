# Co-Creation Lineage Model

## Purpose
Preserve replay-capable provenance across human-AI interaction workflows.

## Lineage Layers
- original human input
- AI-generated output
- human modification
- intervention events
- governance enforcement state
- final operational artifact

## Authority Principles
Human authority remains final authority.

AI participation is preserved as interaction evidence rather than autonomous authorship.

## Replay Objectives
Allow reconstruction of:
- what changed
- who changed it
- when intervention occurred
- what governance conditions existed
- which artifact became operational

## Operational Relevance
Supports:
- audit reconstruction
- governance verification
- regulated workflow traceability
- accountability continuity
- behavioral systems analysis
