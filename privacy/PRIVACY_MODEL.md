# Privacy Model

## Objectives
Preserve behavioral and governance structure while minimizing unnecessary personal exposure.

## Protections
- identifier minimization
- optional anonymization
- replay-safe redaction
- export review controls
- governance-bound evidence handling

## Retained Structures
- behavioral continuity
- intervention timing
- governance telemetry
- authority transitions
- lineage continuity

## Excluded Data
- unnecessary direct identifiers
- credentials
- financial secrets
- unrelated private metadata

## Governance Principle
Replay continuity should preserve governance evidence without requiring unnecessary identity exposure.
