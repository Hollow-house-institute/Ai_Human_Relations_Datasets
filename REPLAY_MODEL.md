# Replay Continuity Model

## Objective
Preserve interaction continuity as replay-capable governance evidence.

## Replay Artifacts
Each replay-capable session may contain:
- transcript.json
- telemetry.json
- annotations.json
- lineage.json
- replay_manifest.json
- checksums.sha256

## Replay Guarantees
- timestamp continuity
- append-only evidence preservation
- governance-state reconstruction
- intervention trace preservation
- deterministic export structure

## Governance Importance
Replay continuity enables:
- longitudinal accountability
- audit reconstruction
- behavioral drift analysis
- runtime governance validation
- intervention effectiveness analysis
